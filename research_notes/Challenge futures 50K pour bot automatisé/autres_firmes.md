## Q0 — Master table (objective): per-firm overnight / weekend / automation / auto-liquidation / 50K price & promo / platforms

No firm on the list is confirmed to allow **weekend holding plus fully automated trading** on CME futures during the evaluation. Three leads are still unresolved: PropEd Capital, Top One Futures and Goat Funded Futures.

### Inferences
- Automation and multi-day holding never come together. Every firm that permits full bots enforces a daily flat between 3:10 PM CT and 5:00 PM ET. The few that allow overnight (The5ers Swing, PropEd) either ban algos or are silent.
- "Overnight allowed" is sometimes only the CME Globex evening session within one trading day (YRM: evening entry, flat by 4:15 PM ET). That does not help a bot that must carry a position through the daily 5 PM ET break.
- Several "overnight/weekend allowed" claims look like mix-ups with sister CFD brands (Goat Funded *Trader* CFD vs Goat Funded *Futures*; Blue Guardian "swap fees"; FundedNext CFD weekend reinstatement). The writer should not count these as futures permissions without official confirmation.

### Gaps
- No official page could be opened. All rows come from secondary summaries.
- Most 50K prices and October 2026 promos are missing (only Topstep, TradeDay, Bulenox, FTMO Futures range, Goat code and TopOne "60% off" were found).
- Platform and Quantower support were not verified for most firms. Only YRM (Quantower, Volumetrica, ATAS), FTMO Futures, TradeDay, Topstep, FTUK and the ProjectX firms surfaced.
- Exact flat times are missing for MFFU, Bulenox, Hyperticks and TickTickTrader.

## Q1 — Which firms (if any) allow BOTH weekend holding AND fully automated trading on CME futures?

### Takeaway
**None confirmed.** The claim "no mainstream/new futures prop firm fits a fully automated multi-day bot" is **mostly confirmed** on available evidence, with three open leads to check on official sites before closing it: **PropEd Capital** (overnight included, weekend paid add-on, automation policy unknown), **Top One Futures** (older reviews claimed overnight+weekend+EAs on all accounts; newer sources say intraday only), and **Goat Funded Futures** (EAs allowed; overnight/weekend conflicting, most specific source says close at 15:55 CT). ## Q4 — Conditions at firms that allow automation (VPS bans, hold-time minimums, HFT definitions, monitoring)

### Takeaway
Where full automation is allowed, typical conditions are: **personal device / no VPS** (Topstep), **sole ownership and exclusivity of the algo** (Tradeify), **no purchased bots or signal services** (TradeDay, TPT if allowed at all), **no latency arbitrage** (Lucid, Goat) and HFT caps. Semi-only firms define HFT by trades per day (Alpha >100, AquaFutures/Blue Guardian/MFFU >200, E8 >300) and add microscalping hold-time rules.

### Cited Findings
- Topstep: TopstepX API automation "requires that it runs from a personal device, prohibiting VPS and VPN usage"; must close by 3:10 PM CT; no HFT/exploitative practices — [pickmytrade TopstepX](https://blog.pickmytrade.io/topstepx-automation-2026.md), [traderspost Topstep](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/automate-topstep-with-traderspost). JWT tokens valid 24 h — same source.
- Tradeify: own algorithm only, exclusively used at Tradeify, not shared or deployed at another prop firm, no HFT, proof of ownership on request (policy checked Sept 2026) — [pickmytrade Tradeify](https://blog.pickmytrade.io/tradeify-webhooks-tradingview-automation/), [tradingfunder Tradeify](https://tradingfunder.com/tradeify-rules).
- Lucid: automation permitted, trader responsible; latency arbitrage banned — [crosstrade Lucid](https://crosstrade.io/learn/prop-firm-ai-trading/lucid-trading), [traderspost Lucid](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/how-to-get-funded-with-lucid-trading).
- Apex: bots in evaluation; on Performance/Live only semi-automation (ATM strategies, webhook alerts with active oversight, trailing stops on manually opened trades) — [quantvps Apex](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/apex-trader-funding-automated-trading-bots), [pickmytrade FAQ](https://pickmytrade.io/faq/prop-firm-automation/).
