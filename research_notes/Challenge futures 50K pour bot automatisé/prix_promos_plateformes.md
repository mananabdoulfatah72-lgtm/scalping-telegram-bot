# 50K futures prop accounts at 30 USD or less (October 2026): prices, promo codes and Quantower compatibility, limited to firms that allow overnight holding, weekend holding and bots

> **How this was researched (read first).** Research date: 2026-10-07. WebFetch was blocked by the network egress proxy for **every** site tried: dontpayfull, canadianfuturestrader, quantvps, propscope, thepropfirmguide, fundedprogramfinder, prop50k, saveonpropfirms, blog.traderspost.io and propfirmmap. The shared web-search budget then ran out (200 calls per turn) before the last checks: the DayTraders Pro fee article, Quantower aggressor data, and DayTraders "$34.90" pricing. **Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries, not from pages I read in full.** Each citation points to the result page(s) the summary drew on. When a summary blended several pages, I cite the most likely one and say so. Treat every price as "reported by aggregator X". Check it on the firm's checkout page before buying. Aggregator "verified" labels and "October 2026" titles are often auto-generated SEO and do not prove a code works.

## Q1: Which 50K accounts can be bought for 30 USD or less today, with which code, and what does it cost in total to get funded? (Objectives A and B)

### Takeaway
**No account I found meets all four conditions at once: 50K, 30 USD or less, overnight and weekend holding, and bots allowed.** The only 50K evaluations reported at 30 USD or less are at **Bulenox**: 19.25 USD with code PROPSCOPE, or about 26.91 USD with KEP91. Bulenox bans overnight and weekend holding, so it is out. **DayTraders.com is the only firm in scope that allows all three (overnight, weekend, bots).** Its cheapest 50K evaluations are the Trail 50K (34.90 USD at 90% off with code FPF, or 52 to 57 USD at 85%) and the Static 50K (about 40 USD at 80%). Either one then needs a **130 USD activation fee**, so the true cost to reach a funded (Pro) account is about **165 to 187 USD**.

### Inferences
- For a fully automated bot that holds overnight and over weekends, **DayTraders is the only option among the firms checked**. ETF Diamond Hands and Phidias Swing allow the holding but ban fully automated trading. Every other firm requires flat positions every day.
- **The 30 USD budget only covers the evaluation fee, never the funded stage.** At DayTraders, even a 90% Trail (34.90 USD) is slightly over budget, and the 130 USD activation brings the total to about 165 USD.
  - The only sub-30 DayTraders cases are hypothetical: a 90% sale on the Static 50K (200 × 0.10 = 20 USD), or a 91% Trail code like January 2026's NEW2026 (349 × 0.09 = 31.41 USD, still just above 30).
  - Activation is only paid after passing, so "30 USD to attempt" is reachable with a strong sale. "30 USD to be funded" is not.
- Bulenox at 19.25 to 26.91 USD is the only true sub-30 USD 50K. It suits an intraday-only version of the bot (flat by 3:59 PM CT, with automation pre-approved), not the overnight/weekend design.
