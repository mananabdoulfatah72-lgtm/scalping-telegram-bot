# Open leads: PropEd Capital, Top One Futures, Goat Funded Futures, and the DayTraders S2F 50K price (as of 2026-10-07)

> **How these notes were gathered.** WebFetch was refused by the egress proxy on every domain tried: propedcapital.com, propfirmmap.com, propscorer.com, fundedtrading.com, forexpeacearmy.com, globenewswire.com, blog.traderspost.io, tradetanto.com, thegodfunded.com, proptradingvibes.com, fundedprogramfinder.com and daytraders.com. So every finding below comes from **WebSearch result summaries** of the cited pages, not from verbatim page reads. Quoted wording is the wording as the search tool reported it. Where the search tool did not say which result a sentence came from, I say so. About 42 searches were used.
>
> **Bottom line across the three leads.** None of the three firms offers weekend holding **and** fully automated trading on a 50K CME futures account, and none does it for ≤ $30.
> - **PropEd Capital** is the only one that allows weekend holding, and only as a paid add-on (about $26 on 50K). Its automation policy is ambiguous: the official Terms allow automated strategies "only where supported and permitted", and third parties contradict each other. Cost is about $105 + $26 = about $131 for 50K.
> - **Top One Futures**: its official help center bans both overnight holding (flat by 4:10 PM EST) and EAs/bots.
> - **Goat Funded Futures** allows the trader's own bots on EOD/Flex/Instant Classic/1-Day-Pass, but its official help center says every position is auto-flattened at 15:55 CT. No overnight, no weekend.
> - **DayTraders S2F 50K**: the best-supported checkout price is **$342 (40% off $570)**. The DamnPropFirms "$57 with DGT" figure is poorly supported (details in section 4).

## 1. PropEd Capital (futures): overnight, weekend add-on, automation, 50K price, drawdown, platform, real CME?

### Takeaway
PropEd includes weekday overnight/swing holds. Weekend holds need a paid add-on, quoted at $16.35 / $26.25 / $42.75 / $54.75 on the four Standard Evaluation sizes, which works out to **$26.25 on 50K** (inferred). The Standard Eval 50K is listed at **$105 (list $175)**, with a **$2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown**, **no daily loss limit**, a $3,000 target and 40% consistency. Trading runs on the Onyx web platform with Rithmic data, plus Rithmic-approved third-party screens, and since August 2026 also a Volumetrica/DXFeed "Nexus" platform. Accounts are simulated on CME-group products. Automation is **not clearly allowed**. The official Terms say automated strategies "may be used only where supported and permitted". PropFirmMap says EAs are permitted. Forex Peace Army says EAs, bots and automated trading are not permitted. Even with a bot allowed, the cost (~$131 for 50K plus weekend) is far above the $30 budget.

### Inferences
- **Weekend add-on on 50K = $26.25.** Each of the four quoted add-on prices is exactly 15% of a list price: $109, $175, $285, $365. The 50K list price is $175, and 15% of that is $26.25. This assumes the four Standard Eval sizes are 25K/50K/100K/150K. Not confirmed on a PropEd page.
- **All-in 50K with weekend holding:** about $105 + $26.25 ≈ **$131** at the price seen, or $175 + $26.25 ≈ $201 at list. Even at the March 2026 55% deal, the evaluation alone is about $79. **More than 4× the $30 budget.**
- "Real CME futures": the market data and products are real CME-group futures (Rithmic/DXFeed feeds), but the accounts are **simulated**, like every firm in this study.
- On automation, the official wording ("only where supported and permitted", trader responsible for orders "by automation") reads as conditional permission, not a ban. A Rithmic-approved third-party platform (e.g. NinjaTrader or Quantower on Rithmic) could technically run a bot. Whether an unsupervised bot holding over a weekend is "supported and permitted" needs a written answer from PropEd support.
- With no daily loss limit and an EOD trailing drawdown, PropEd's risk rules suit a multi-day bot structurally better than most firms. The 40% consistency rule still penalises lumpy overnight gains.

### Gaps
- No page states PropEd's automation policy per stage (evaluation vs funded) or per platform (Onyx vs Rithmic third-party vs Nexus/Volumetrica).
- The Friday cutoff time without the add-on was not found.
- Whether the weekend add-on is priced on list or discounted price, and whether it is available on funded accounts, was not found.
- No October 2026 promo code for PropEd was found.
- No page confirms the exact Standard Eval sizes (needed to pin the $26.25 add-on to 50K).

## 2. Top One Futures: overnight/weekend in 2026, automation, 50K price/promo, drawdown

### Takeaway
The **official help center** settles it: **no overnight holding (flat by 4:10 PM EST, forced liquidation otherwise), no weekend trading, and no EAs or bots** ("manual, skill-based trading"). Third-party pages that say "overnight, weekends and EAs allowed on all accounts" or "weekend holding allowed on Elite, Elite Access, Ignite, Instant Sim Funded" are outdated or wrong. The cheapest 50K entry is Elite Access, with a one-time fee of $39 per the official site. It has a $2,000 EOD trailing drawdown, no daily loss limit in evaluation and a $1,000 daily loss limit once funded. **Lead closed.**

### Cited Findings
- Official: "holding trades overnight is not allowed — all trades must be closed by 4:10 PM EST daily". Failure to close "may face forced liquidation of trades by the system". Weekend trading is "strictly prohibited". The trading window runs 6:00 PM EST to 4:10 PM EST the next day, Monday to Friday. — [Top One Futures help: Can I Hold Trades Overnight?](https://help.toponefutures.com/en/articles/10907901-can-i-hold-trades-overnight); [Top One Futures help: Trading Hours](https://help.toponefutures.com/en/articles/11165893-trading-hours-at-top-one-futures)
- Official: "they do not allow the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) or Bots on their accounts … require all traders to engage in manual, skill-based trading". Consequences: "account suspension or termination, loss of funding privileges". — [Top One Futures help: Can I Use EA's/Bots?](https://help.toponefutures.com/en/articles/10907920-can-i-use-ea-s-bots); [Top One Futures help: Prohibited Trading Practices](https://help.toponefutures.com/en/articles/11021584-prohibited-trading-practices)
- **Contradicting third parties (older or wrong):** "news trading, EAs, and overnight holds are allowed across all five accounts" — [LuxAlgo Top One Futures](https://www.luxalgo.com/prop-firms/top-one-futures/). "Weekend holding … is allowed on Elite, Elite Access, Ignite, and Instant Sim Funded, though S2F Sim PRO has specific weekend-hold rules" — attributed by search to [PropTradingVibes rules overview](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/top-one-futures-rules-overview). Yet another PropTradingVibes page summary says "Weekend holding is NOT permitted. Overnight holding is generally NOT permitted" — [PropTradingVibes account types](https://proptradingvibes.com/blog/top-one-futures-account-types). TradeTanto's summary says both "EAs and bots are allowed within specified limits" and "automated trading/bots are prohibited" — [TradeTanto Top One rules](https://tradetanto.com/learn/top-one-futures-rules)
- TradersPost says Top One connects "through ProjectX and Tradovate", but "the firm's automation policy varies by account type, and some may restrict direct bot attachment or require manual trading" — [TradersPost: Automate TopOneFutures](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/automate-top-one-futures-with-traderspost)

- **50K price and rules (Elite Access):** "one-time entry fee of $39". Landing page title: "Trade Up to $150K for $39". 50K: EOD trailing drawdown $2,000. No daily loss limit in evaluation; $1,000 daily loss limit when funded. 6% profit target. So the restrictive version is the current one, and the permissive third-party claims most likely describe an earlier period or a different brand (Top One Trader CFD).

## 3. Goat Funded Futures: EAs, overnight/weekend on any plan, 50K price, code PFC

### Takeaway
The **official help center** says **no overnight and no weekend holding on any plan**. Anything open is auto-flattened at **15:55 CT** (EOD, Flex, Instant Classic, Daily Payouts; third parties add Sprint). The trader's **own EAs and automated strategies are allowed** on EOD, 1-Day-Pass, Flex and Instant Classic. They are banned on Daily Payouts and Instant Lite. 50K EOD is **$69 (from $138)**; 50K Flex is $109 (from $218). Code PFC (30%) was not confirmed in this pass; 50% codes (TRUSTED, SUMMER50) are reported. **Lead closed**: automation yes, but intraday only.

### Cited Findings
- Official: the EOD Challenge is "a one-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown and no time limit. Positions must be closed before the market closes." "Any position still open is closed automatically at 15:55 CT, five minutes before the session ends." The same auto-flatten appears in the EOD, Flex, Instant Classic and Daily Payouts spec articles. — [Goat help: EOD Challenge specs](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14095302-eod-challenge-specifications-rules-and-payouts); [Goat help: Flex Challenge specs](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14888545-flex-challenge-specifications-rules-and-payouts); [Goat help: Instant Classic](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14095625-instant-classic-specifications-rules-and-payouts); [Goat help: Drawdown & Trading Rules](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/collections/19048110-drawdown-trading-rules)

"Daily Payouts and Instant Lite are manual trading products. Bots, Expert Advisors and automated strategies are not permitted on either", nor grid, martingale or HFT. Copying your own trades across your own accounts is allowed. — [Goat help: Signals, copy trading and automated strategies](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/15945150-signals-copy-trading-and-automated-strategies); [Goat help: Daily Payouts](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/16863129-daily-payouts-specifications-rules-and-payouts)

Flex "$109 (from $218)", target $3,000, max drawdown $2,000, no daily loss limit and no consistency rule once funded. — [Goat help: EOD Challenge](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14095302-eod-challenge-specifications-rules-and-payouts); [Goat help: Flex Challenge](https://help.goatfundedfutures.com/en/articles/14888545-flex-challenge-specifications-rules-and-payouts)
- Products: CME, COMEX, NYMEX and CBOT futures on simulated accounts — [Goat Funded Futures](https://goatfundedfutures.com/)
- Codes: TRUSTED "saves you 50% on all plans". SUMMER50 gives up to 50%. "The $50K EOD plan with a 50% discount drops to roughly $34.50." — [TheTrustedProp: Goat promo codes 2026](https://thetrustedprop.com/blogs/goat-funded-futures-free-trials-promo-codes-how-to-access-discounted-challenges-2026). Code PFC (30%) was found only in the earlier note (propfirmscom); this pass did not confirm it.

### Inferences
- 50K EOD at $69 with 30% off (PFC) would be about $48.30. At 50% off it would be about $34.50. Both are above $30, and the plan forces a daily flat anyway.
- Goat is a good fit for a fully automated **intraday** MNQ bot that is flat by 15:55 CT. It is not a fit for overnight or weekend holding.

### Gaps
